Fix one The pair disagreed about what "starved" means.

One half reported what the other half had dismissed

section_carries_its_body names the starved section; no_foreign_section_start_in_body names the leaf that ate it. The first has skipped repealed sections since it was written, and its docstring says why: an omitted section is legitimately empty, so an empty one is not evidence of anything.

The second one never asked. It tested the victim with _body_beyond_heading(victim) alone — which returns "" for a repealed section for exactly the same reason it returns "" for a starved one.

What that looks like on the Customs Act, in seven consecutive editions: 

section  2: ... such other 20[officers of Customs as may be notified by the Board;]
section 20: 15[20. 112[Omitted]

The 20 inside section 2 is an amendment marker, not a section code. The permissive start detector is meant to see it — its looseness is deliberate, and the five surrounding conditions are what filter it out. Condition four is the one that should have: "the victim leaf carries no body of its own, which is the signature of the binding failure." Section 20 carries no body because Parliament omitted it. Nothing was eaten.

The fix is the guard the twin already has, reusing the same predicate:

            if _is_omission(victim):
                continue          # legitimately empty: nothing was there to eat

Measured on identical JSON — no document was re-converted — 64 → 54.

The check is narrowed, not switched off. The lock asserts both directions: the omitted victim is skipped, and a victim that is merely heading-only is still reported. With the guard removed the self-check fails.

It made one hit more honest rather than fewer

The invariant reports at most one hit per leaf, then breaks. On Sales Tax 15.9.2021 the first match inside section 2 was the omitted 3A; with that skipped the scan continues and finds 189[3B. Collection of ex… — section 3B, a real starved leaf and one of the five in that document's stub block. The count for that document did not change. What it points at did.

Fix two An exemption that covered one invariant and not its mirror.

Same document, same cause, only half written down

Round 3 exempted section_carries_its_body on the two compilations — Customs Rules 2001, whose contents page is an index of 44 separately-notified S.R.O. rule sets, and Federal Excise Rules 2005, which starts a second instrument at PDF page 75 with its own contents page. Both were traced to the source PDF, with the deletion of those entries as the Phase 5 gate.

The mirror invariant fires on the same two documents from the same cause:

Customs Rules 2001   rule 3  body holds  '7. Jurisdiction _____________________'
                     rule 5  body holds  '6 Annexure-E.2 Legal Compliance YES YES YES'
                     rule 1  body holds  '15.06.2002.'
Federal Excise 2005  rule 1  body holds  '2. Definitions.--1n these Rules, unless ...'

An index row with its leaders, an annexure table row, an S.R.O. date read as rule 15, and the real opening of the second instrument. Every victim is one of the index-row or contents-row leaves already covered above — so the two invariants were reporting the two halves of one defect and only one half had an entry.

Two entries, the same traced evidence, the same deletion condition — 54 → 50. The exempted hits still run and are still printed with their counts: an exemption documents a failure, it does not hide its size.

wip/tasks.md's Phase 5 gate said "four entries" and then enumerated three. It is now five, and counted.
