Five schema families

The parser chose how to read a document from the folder it was filed in. Measured across all 190 documents, that assumption fails on four separate axes — and one of them refuses nine editions of a live ordinance outright.

01 What changed

Parsing behaviour used to come from the lane. Now it comes from what the document measurably is.

Before

The directory decides. Three lanes, three parsing profiles, selected by which folder a PDF was filed in.

acts/ → legal_ingest + ACTS rules/ → legal_ingest + RULES ordinance/ → fbr_ingest (a separate fork)

After

The document decides. One pdftotext pass, twenty measured fields, five ordered families — each carrying the evidence that chose it.

measure() → classify() → profile 0 of 190 documents unexplained
190documents measured, up from 162 staged
5families explain the whole corpus

02 The four ways one cap failed

Two unrelated instrument kinds shared a lane

An amending statute is not a consolidated one. Its leaves are directives that quote a different law. 36 documents are amending — 25 filed under acts, 11 under ordinance — and all 36 were being parsed as compilations.

Before · The Tax Laws (Amendment) Act, 2024

The Gazette masthead was parsed as a chapter of the Act. Its four real clauses hung beneath a publication label.

chapter code = "PART I" heading = "Acts, Ordinances, President's Orders and Regulations"

After

Masthead suppressed. The instrument now names the three laws it changes, which is the one fact that makes it navigable.

family = amending (1.00) amends = Sales Tax Act, 1990 Federal Excise Act, 2005 Income Tax Ordinance, 2001

The ordinance lane had only ever seen one law

26 of its 42 inventory documents were never staged. The nine flat, contents-less Islamabad Capital Territory (Tax on Services) Ordinance editions route to fbr_ingest, which has no body-driven fallback.

Before · today's routing

Refuses to convert. Not a bad parse — no parse at all, for every one of the nine editions.

RuntimeError: TOC parse left 3 section(s) without a chapter container: refusing to drop them.

After · same file, --profile auto

Classified consolidated, routed to the pipeline that already handles contents-less editions, and converted.

family consolidated (0.50) pages=15 chapters=1 sections=3

The parser was never broken for these documents. The routing was. That is the single clearest argument for choosing a profile from evidence rather than from a folder name.

Shape varies inside a lane — and holds across editions

The Customs Act prints 42 chapter lines in 15 of its 20 editions, spanning 2007 to 2025. Structure inside a document group is remarkably stable, which is what makes a family a useful unit at all.

But eras break inside a group

The Income Tax Ordinance 2001 is one law across thirteen text-bearing editions. At the 30 June 2024 edition the publisher re-typeset it, and the chapter-line count jumps twentyfold.

And one negative finding worth as much as the rest: “Finance Acts” is a filing folder, not a document group. Twenty files, four container shapes. Grouping by folder name would have manufactured a family out of a filing convention.

Why the amending test needs two signals

Amendment-language density alone is diluted by anything an instrument reproduces at length. A second signal — clause headings that name their target — carries the two documents density misses, and admits no false positive across all 144 text-bearing documents.

04 The node model

Before

No type field existed anywhere in the output. A chapter, a schedule part and a section leaf were the same dict shape, told apart by which keys happened to be present.

Identity was an array position: /chapters/0/sections/3. Insert a node above a leaf and every id below it is re-minted.

After

type on every node — toc.Node.kind always computed it and serialisation always discarded it.

node_key is the ancestor chain by code, sitting beside the positional key and changing no existing id.

ch:vii/pt:i/s:114

Leaf-level diff and resync are not solved here, and were not meant to be. The point of doing discovery first is that the identity scheme built next does not silently inherit the one-cap-per-lane assumption.

05 What this deliberately does not build

Not builtBecause
A stratified samplerA full census over all 190 documents measures ~26 s. A sampler to avoid 26 seconds is more code than it saves, and it would hide the edition drift the report exists to show.
A clustering algorithm190 points on 20 hand-picked features needs numpy, is threshold-sensitive, and the clusters get hand-labelled anyway. A Counter group-by is the evidence a human reads; five ordered predicates are what code runs.
A families JSON schema and loaderA family carries a profile, and a profile is behaviour — so families are dataclasses, like the rest of this repo's behaviour. The measurements are the JSON, because those are what must diff on a rerun.
A post-OCR SRO familyNot observable in any text layer today. The hypothesis is recorded as corroborating signals on no_text_layer, to be measured after OCR rather than guessed at now.
An “ambiguous” stateBuilt, measured, deleted. With ordered predicates a later match is always expected, so it flagged 29 documents where nothing was wrong.

06 Evidence that nothing regressed

The change is meant to be invisible to every consolidated document and visible only where the corpus says it should be.

These 243 hits are the pre-existing anomaly register, re-measured. It reads higher than the 157 previously recorded because PR #41 added an invariant and tightened another, and because much of the on-disk JSON predates several parser fixes — not because of anything on this branch.

19 of them are no_foreign_section_start_in_body, and that invariant says “a leaf contains the start of another section in its body”. That is the literal signature of an amending instrument parsed as a consolidated one: the other section is the one being quoted. Those should be re-measured after re-conversion under the family profiles, not patched.

Byte-level check on a consolidated act

Federal Excise Act, 2005 (as amended to 01.07.2017), converted both ways: --profile auto output is identical to lane routing once the new type, node_key and metadata.family keys are removed.

Reproduce the whole thing

python tools/stage_corpus.py --from "<FBR repo root>"
python tools/discover_corpus.py --write        # 190 docs, ~26s
python tools/discover_corpus.py --check        # no drift
python tools/discover_corpus.py --assert       # 190 documents, 0 problems
python tools/discover_corpus.py --reconcile --inventory <xlsx>
python tools/discover_corpus.py --verify-lanes # 73 route differently
python tools/run_tests_smoke.py                # 13 self-checks

--check is wired into the pipeline gate, so a family threshold that drifts away from the corpus fails the build instead of silently classifying wrongly. A missing corpus is a SKIP, exactly as the lane suites already are.
