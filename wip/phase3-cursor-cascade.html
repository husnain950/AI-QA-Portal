Every entry in the block prints about six pages ahead of its own contents page — 3A at 31/33 against an expected 39, s.4 at 34 against 40, s.7 at 37 against 43. The block is internally consistent; the TOC is what is off. Nothing interleaves.

Section 3 is the one entry whose code opens two body lines: page 28, its real heading six pages ahead exactly like its neighbours, and page 37, a later cross-reference. It took 37, the monotonic cursor jumped to 606, and the next seven entries all print before that.

The cause The ladder stops at the first tolerance that hits.

The nearer candidate is reached before the further right one

        for tol in (2, 4, 8):
            near = [p for p in positions
                    if abs(body_refs[p].page - expected) <= tol]
            if near:
                pos = min(near, key=lambda p: (abs(body_refs[p].page - expected), p))
                break

|37 − 34| = 3 and |28 − 34| = 6. At tol=2 neither is near; at tol=4 only 37 is, and the loop breaks. The ladder never sees 28.

The rolling drift_window exists for exactly this drift — but it is empty here. Sections 1 and 2 never open a body line at all, so s.3 is the first entry to resolve and the drift is 0.

And the ordering guard below the ladder cannot see it either:

if nxt is not None and body_refs[pos].page > expected_page(nxt) + drift + 8:
    continue

It rejects a match past where the next entry is expected — 39 + 8 = 47, and 37 is well inside. It cannot see where the next entry actually prints, which is 31.

The fix Look ahead one entry, before the ladder rather than after.

A candidate that starves the next entry loses to one that doesn't

        if nxt is not None and len(positions) > 1:
            nxt_positions = code_positions.get(nxt.code, ())
            viable = [p for p in positions if any(q > p for q in nxt_positions)]
            if viable:
                positions = viable

The cascade is knowable at the moment of the choice rather than seven entries later. It is a tie-break between candidates and never a rejection: with one candidate it does not run, and where every candidate starves the next entry — its code may not open a body line at all, the ordinary case for an omitted section — viable is empty and the ladder decides exactly as before.

Two blind tools Both were named in the process; neither could run it.

why_unbuilt.py was measuring rules documents as Acts

It called calibrate(pdf) and parse_toc(lines) with no profile, so every RULES document got the Acts folio, leader, ordinal and codeless-row settings — wrong page offset, wrong TOC row count, and therefore a cascade reported at the wrong entry. The one diagnostic written for this class of defect could not be trusted on the lane holding 20 of the remaining 44 hits. It now takes --lane. (Caught while fixing it: calibrate's second positional is sample, not profile — the pipeline passes it by keyword.)
