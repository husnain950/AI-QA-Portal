The blockers are fixed and the marathon is deferred: every document that needs no OCR has been re-converted, in all three lanes, at one parser revision. The blocker that had to land first

families.py hardcoded consolidated → ACTS, and convert.py passed profile=None, which overrode the partial(pipeline.run, profile=RULES) binding in rules_ingest. Every consolidated rules document would have re-converted as an Act, across twelve fields of parsing behaviour.

The model was already written down in both docstrings — a Profile is what the PRINTER does; a Family is what the document IS — and the code contradicted it. A family meaning “an ordinary consolidated statute” cannot know whether that statute is printed the Acts way or the Rules way. Only the lane knows. So the family’s profile became an override: consolidated names none and keeps what the lane bound, and amending is the only family that overrides anything. Refusal moved to a separate Family.parseable, because profile=None had been carrying both meanings at once — which is exactly why consolidated named a profile in the first place.

$ tools/convert.py rules "…/AML_CFT Sanction Rules, 2020.pdf" --profile auto
[rules] family consolidated (confidence 0.33) -> profile rules
…
instrument_kind = rules | notified_by = S.R.O. 950(I)/2020

notified_by is a RULES-only behaviour. Under the old resolution this document would have been parsed as an Act and lost it.

Findings

1
Data loss

Two Sales Tax Act editions the current parser refuses

Sales Tax Act,1990 as amended up to 30.06.2020 and The Sales Tax Act, 1990 (as amended up to 31st December, 2019) both fail with TOC parse left 2 section(s) without a chapter container. Both had JSON; it is now in output/_refused/, and the acts lane reads 78.

This is not the 80 → 78 the pre-flight predicted. That one was about two no_text_layer documents being refused before OCR — and it did not happen, because both are scans and were never attempted. These are different documents and a different cause: the JSON on disk was written by an older revision that could convert them.

Controlled: both classify consolidated at confidence 1.0, so they resolve to ACTS — the same profile the lane binds — and --profile lane raises the identical error. Parser drift, not the profile. Highest-value Phase 3 item, because it costs the corpus documents rather than hits.

2
Defect + false positive

One spurious chapter, nineteen documents

tree chapter codes: ['CHAPTER 1', 'CHAPTER I', 'CHAPTER II', ...]
                      ^^^^^^^^^ 'PRELIMINARY', 0 sections
                                  duplicate of 'CHAPTER I' 'PRELIMINARY', 2 sections

insert_missing_body_chapters reads a body line printing the numeral as an Arabic 1, finds no roman I to match, and inserts a second, empty PRELIMINARY chapter. That is also why every Customs edition went 22 → 23 chapters. All nineteen body_chapters_in_tree hits on the acts lane are this one cause.

The invariant reporting it is half-blind too: _tree_chapter_numeral returns None for a non-roman numeral and drops it from the set it compares against. On the rules lane the same pair disagrees the other way — "CHAPTER VIA" normalises to VI-A on the tree side and VIA on the body side — producing 2 hits on chapters that are present.

3
Hypothesis falsified

The amending instruments were not the foreign-section hits

Both planning documents argued that no_foreign_section_start_in_body is “the literal signature of an amending instrument parsed as a consolidated one”, and that Phase 2 might close those hits with no parser change. Measured: the seven amending instruments that converted are clean on that invariant — clean on everything, in fact, bar one clause_codes_plausible hit on Finance Act 2024.

All ten acts hits sit in consolidated statutes: seven Customs Act editions (2019–2025, one each) and three Sales Tax Act editions. The hypothesis survives only for the eighteen amending instruments still behind OCR, and it can no longer be the reason to schedule that class first.

4
Measurement

Three of the register’s eight classes were dormant, not absent

inv_body_chapters_in_tree reads metadata.body_chapter_numerals, and its docstring makes it a deliberate no-op on JSON without the key “so old output does not fail the lane until it is reconverted”. The old JSON predates the key, so the invariant never ran. Re-conversion woke it up: 31 hits the previous register could not see, across body_chapters_in_tree, section_codes_ordered and structure_counts.

That is the argument for re-converting at one revision, stated as a number.

5
Cost of skipping OCR

Seven amending instruments, not twenty-five

Seven documents now carry instrument_kind="amending" — the first time anything in this corpus has been parsed as the amending instrument it is. The other eighteen are blocked by scanned pages, and two of them by one page each: Finance Act 2022 (952 pages, one scanned cover) and Finance Act 2023.

6
Handled

The ordinance lane would have doubled itself

Not one of its twelve JSONs was named what convert_all.out_path would name it — they are hand-normalised against differently-spelled sources, though their metadata.filename matches the staged PDFs. Converting the lane produced 21 files for 12 editions.

Resolved after the run rather than before, so a failed conversion could not cost an edition: each old name was matched to a new JSON by metadata.filename and only then retired, with every file still banked in output/_pre_phase2/. Nine pairs matched — structurally identical, down to the count of sections, chapters and schedules — and three old files with no re-converted twin were kept.
